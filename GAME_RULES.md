# UNO Show 'Em No Mercy — Rules

## 1. Setup
- Players: 2–6.
- Shuffle all 168 cards.
- Deal 7 cards to each player.
- Place the remaining cards face down as the draw pile.
- Turn over the top card to start the discard pile. If it is an action card, turn over another card.
- The player to the dealer's left starts. Play proceeds clockwise.

## 2. How to Play
- On your turn, play one card matching the top discard card by color, number, or symbol.
- Wild cards can generally be played regardless of the current color.
- If you have no playable card, draw cards until you draw one you can play, then play it.
- Follow the effect of any action card you play.
- When you have one card left, shout "UNO!"
- If another player catches you failing to call UNO before the next player starts their turn, draw 2 cards.

## 3. Special Rules

### Stacking Draw Cards
- When a player plays a draw card (+2, +4, +6, or +10), the next player can stack a draw card of equal or higher value.
- The penalty accumulates and passes to the next player.
- The first player unable to stack must draw the entire accumulated penalty and loses their turn.
- Example: +4 → +6 → +10 = the next player must draw 20 cards unless they can stack another qualifying draw card.

### The 7 Rule — Swap Hands
- Whenever you play a 7, you must swap your entire hand with another player of your choice.

### The 0 Rule — Pass Hands
- Whenever you play a 0, every player passes their entire hand to the next player in the current direction of play.

### Mercy Rule — 25 Cards
- If you ever have 25 or more cards in your hand, you are immediately eliminated from the game.
- Your cards are removed from play.

## 4. Action Cards

| Card | Effect |
|---|---|
| Draw 2 (+2) | The next player draws 2 cards and loses their turn. |
| Draw 4 (+4) | The next player draws 4 cards and loses their turn. |
| Skip | The next player loses their turn. |
| Reverse | Changes the direction of play. With 2 players, it acts as a Skip. |
| Discard All | Discard all cards in your hand matching the played card's color, including the card just played. |
| Skip Everyone | Skip all other players and take another turn. |
| Wild Draw 6 (+6) | Choose the continuing color. The next player draws 6 cards and loses their turn. |
| Wild Draw 10 (+10) | Choose the continuing color. The next player draws 10 cards and loses their turn. |
| Wild Reverse Draw 4 | Reverse direction. The next player in the new direction draws 4 cards and loses their turn. |
| Wild Color Roulette | The next player chooses a color, then reveals cards from the draw pile until they find that color. They take all revealed cards and lose their turn. |

## 5. Winning the Game

There are two ways to win:

1. **Empty your hand:** Be the first player to play your final card.
2. **Eliminate everyone else:** All other players reach 25 or more cards and are eliminated.

If the draw pile runs out, reshuffle the discard pile (leaving its top card in place) to create a new draw pile.

## 6. Quick Reminders
- Match by color, number, or symbol.
- Call UNO when you have one card remaining.
- Stack draw cards to pass accumulated penalties.
- Playing a 7 forces a hand swap.
- Playing a 0 makes everyone pass hands.
- Never reach 25 cards!
- First to empty their hand wins, unless everyone else is eliminated first.
